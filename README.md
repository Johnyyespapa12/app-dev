# Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

## Overview

**Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood** is a *dark fantasy adventure* anime based on the manga by **Hiromu Arakawa**. It follows two brothers who try to bring back their mother using alchemy, and pay a heavy price for it.

---

## Quick Facts

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Studio | Bones |
| Episodes | 64 |
| Aired | 2009 – 2010 |
| Genre | Action, Adventure, Fantasy |

## Main Characters

1. **Edward Elric** – the young State Alchemist known as the Fullmetal Alchemist
2. **Alphonse Elric** – Edward's younger brother, whose soul is bound to a suit of armor
3. **Roy Mustang** – the Flame Alchemist and a military colonel

## Why I Like It

- Great story with very few filler episodes
- Strong themes about family, sacrifice, and responsibility
- Memorable characters, even the side ones
- ~~Boring parts~~ none, honestly

## Core Idea

> Alchemy in this world follows the rule of equivalent exchange: to gain something, you must give up something of equal value.
